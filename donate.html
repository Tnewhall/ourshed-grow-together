The Grow Together Initiative

Helping more gardens take root.

OurShed's goal goes beyond selling small-space gardening kits. We want schools and community groups with fewer resources to have a chance to grow, learn and build together.

Current status: This is a planned program. We're not collecting money or tools yet, and we have not named any recipient partners.
10:1Our proposed commitment: for every ten GrowBox kits sold, fund one basic gardening kit for a school or community group.
Where support would go

A purpose for every contribution.

If a donation program is launched, contributions would go toward the following needs. These are intended uses, not a finalized allocation or an active fundraising campaign.

🪵

Materials and hardware

Precut wood, fasteners, liners and other essential parts for basic beginner-friendly kits provided to qualifying community recipients.

🌱

Growing supplies

Where suitable, potting mix, seeds and simple starter materials to help recipients begin using donated kits.

📦

Delivery and access

Packaging, local transport and clear instructions needed to make donated kits practical to receive and use.

No donation percentages, tax-deductibility, nonprofit status, or guaranteed recipient commitments have been established. We will publish the final terms and accounting approach before accepting contributions.
What the model could look like

Support built into the business.

Our proposed sales-funded 10:1 model is separate from any future voluntary donation program. The goal is to make access part of how OurShed grows, not an afterthought.

  • Sales-funded: Ten GrowBox kits sold would trigger funding for one basic community kit.
  • Voluntary support: A future donation option may help fund additional kits and delivery costs.
  • Accountability: Once operational, we intend to report kits funded and delivered, recipient partners, and what funds were used for.
