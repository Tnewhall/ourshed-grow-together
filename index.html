Growing guides

A good start goes a long way.

A few basics worth knowing before you plant anything in a small space.

☀️

Start with sunlight

Track how many hours of direct sun your spot gets. Leafy greens can tolerate less than many fruiting plants.

💧

Give water a way out

Containers need drainage and suitable potting mix. Avoid leaving roots sitting in water.

🥬

Choose the right plants

Herbs, lettuce, and compact varieties are good plants to research for limited space. Check root depth and local conditions.

Our story & SDG 9

Good projects should be easier to start.

OurShed began with a simple idea: make DIY gardening easier for people without a big yard or a big budget.

Questions

Questions? We have answers.

Can I purchase a GrowBox right now?

Not yet. GrowBox is in development. This website presents a concept and doesn't collect orders or payments.

Is the $44.99 price final?

No. That's a proposed retail price. Materials, manufacturing, packaging, and shipping still need to be validated.

Will GrowBox work on my balcony?

It depends on your building rules and the balcony's rated load. A filled planter can be heavy, so always confirm weight limits, placement restrictions, and drainage requirements.

Has the Grow Together donation program started?

No. It's a planned initiative that would start after the business is selling products and has verified community partnerships.
