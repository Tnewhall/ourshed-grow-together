How it could work

Simple from the first step.

Our design goal is to take the intimidating parts out of building a planter. These steps describe the intended experience, not tested instructions.

01

Find your space

Choose a suitable outdoor location with the light your plants need and a surface that can safely support the full planter.

02

Connect the panels

Use labeled precut components and reusable connectors. We're working toward clear, beginner-friendly assembly.

03

Start growing

Add the appropriate soil and plants, with drainage and watering suited to your growing conditions.

Growing guides

A good start goes a long way.

A few basics worth knowing before you plant anything in a small space.

☀️

Start with sunlight

Track how many hours of direct sun your spot gets. Leafy greens can tolerate less than many fruiting plants.

💧

Give water a way out

Containers need drainage and suitable potting mix. Avoid leaving roots sitting in water.

🥬

Choose the right plants

Herbs, lettuce, and compact varieties are good plants to research for limited space. Check root depth and local conditions.

Our story & SDG 9

Making useful ideas more accessible.

OurShed began as a student business concept around beginner-friendly DIY projects. We shifted toward affordable modular planters after exploring material costs, competition, and what smaller-space gardeners actually need.

Our direction connects with United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure by exploring standardized kit design, practical small-scale production, and a model intended to make the benefits of that innovation more accessible.

This is an early-stage concept. Our next steps are prototyping, testing, pricing validation, and identifying community partners.

Questions

Good things take groundwork.

Can I purchase a GrowBox right now?

Not yet. GrowBox is in development. This website presents a concept and doesn't collect orders or payments.

Is the $44.99 price final?

No. That's a proposed retail price. Materials, manufacturing, packaging, and shipping still need to be validated.

Will GrowBox work on my balcony?

It depends on your building rules and the balcony's rated load. A filled planter can be heavy, so always confirm weight limits, placement restrictions, and drainage requirements.

Has the Grow Together donation program started?

No. It's a planned initiative that would start after the business is selling products and has verified community partnerships.
